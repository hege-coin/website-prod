Hegeville High

Home to tomorrow's brightest.

Description

Hegeville High is a private high-school in Hegeville.

It is Hendrix's main stage. This is where his charm has a chance to truly shine, affecting students and professors alike, despite him holding the record for most grades repeated in the history of this private school. He mostly enrolled to keep an eye on Hegena and to make fun of Hege and the other “pipsqueaks”, at the expense of his father’s pockets.

Hegeville High is a school otherwise filled with brilliant young minds, including those of Hege and Hegena. Its halls are home to tomorrow’s brightest, but also a breeding ground for drama and questionable life decisions.
