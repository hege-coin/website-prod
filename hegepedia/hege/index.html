Description

Hege is an anthropomorphic brown hedgehog and the central character of the Hege Universe.

He is portrayed as an unassuming, shy, and somewhat clueless hedgehog, whose life took a tragic turn after his parents were ran over and killed in a road accident.

Hege has a deep crush on Hegena, and believes that the only way he can get her attention and love is to become the ultimate meme.

Development

As the The Hege Story progresses, Hege gradually becomes intertwined with the wider world around him.

His experiences, choices, and encounters form the foundation of the story, while his relationships with the people around him introduce much of the universe’s recurring cast.

His journey serves as the primary thread connecting the different stories, characters, and locations of the Hege Universe.

His story is ongoing, and further chapters will continue to reveal more about his character, history, and life.

Social Media

Although a lot has been posted about him , Hege himself has so far been mostly silent on social media.

Not entirely though. For example, leading up to the release of A New Start (L1.C1) he posted a life update to X. [1]

He also has a Reddit account, where he goes by the name “SirHegeALot” (it’s still unclear exactly why he chose that name). [2]
