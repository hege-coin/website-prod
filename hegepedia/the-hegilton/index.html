The Hegilton Hotel

The Hegilton Hotel is a grand and prestigious establishment, known for its impeccable service, highly exclusive dining room, and somewhat unusual clientele.

Description

The Hegilton occupies an enormous building filled with lavish interiors and elaborate furnishings, with the unmistakable sense of grandeur permeating the entire maze of corridors, stairs, chambers and eccentric amenities.

Private lounges, terraces, ballrooms, pools and then art, leisure and intellectual areas. The sheer scale of the Hegilton main building and its grounds makes it feel more like a palace than a conventional hotel, granting the Hegilton the reputation of being a world of its own.

Inside, there always seems to be another room to discover.

Beneath the polished exterior, however, the Hegilton has its own eccentricities.

“Efficiency” is the word of the day, every day, at any cost. The vigil gaze of the managers follows the staff as they operate according to a complicated set of routines, unwritten rules, favors, connections, and survival tactics that are rarely apparent to the guests.

The Hegilton’s Color System

The Hegilton’s many departments each have their own color, making it possible to tell who’s who at a glance. Over the years, even regulars have come to recognize the colors as readily as the staff themselves.

Wellness — Sage
The staff responsible for looking after guests’ comfort and wellbeing, working across the Hegilton’s spa, sauna, pool, gym and other wellness services.
Staff Administration / Personnel Management — Dusty Rose
The people behind the people, handling recruitment, staff matters and the endless business of keeping the Hegilton’s workforce in order.
Waiters — Dark Wine
The dining room’s front line, expected to know every table, every preference and every regular by name. Few parts of the hotel demand quite so much grace under pressure.
Lobby Staff — Teal
Receptionists, porters, concierges and the various faces guests encounter upon arriving at the Hegilton. They tend to know where everything is and, more importantly, who to ask.
Security — Charcoal
Responsible for keeping the hotel’s guests, staff and more sensitive corners under watch. Their presence is usually quiet, but rarely unnoticed.
Bar crew — Navy
The staff behind the bars and lounges, accustomed to long nights and keepers of the Hegilton After Dark.
Managers — Plum
The ones keeping a watchful eye over the hotel’s many moving parts. They are expected to keep things running smoothly, whatever it takes.
Housekeeping — Warm Grey
The staff responsible for keeping the Hegilton immaculate from top to bottom. They know the rooms, corridors and service passages better than almost anyone.
Gardeners — Olive Brown
Responsible for tending the Hegilton’s extensive grounds, gardens and outdoor spaces. Their territory extends well beyond the hotel’s walls.
Casino employees — Green
Green for good luck.

List of Amenities Inside the Main Building

Basement

  • Wine cellar: an extensive collection of wines is kept beneath the hotel, reserved for the Hegilton’s most distinguished guests.
  • Staff lockers/changing rooms.
  • Security/CCTV.
  • Laundry and pressing room.
  • Mechanical, electrical, boiler room.

Ground Floor

  • Reception, Lobby and Atrium.
  • Lounge Bar.
  • Dining room.
  • Private dining room.
  • Billiards den.
  • Museum: the museum houses historic artifacts and curiosities held by the families of Hegilton guests for generations, some of which remain in their possession under agreements allowing them to be loaned for public exhibition.
  • Art gallery: open to guests and visitors, the gallery displays rotating collections of paintings, sculptures, and other works from the personal collections of Hegilton guests.
  • Jewelry boutique and vault: A private jewelry boutique and private vault where the hotel’s more ostentatious guests can securely store and showcase their gems and valuables.
  • Library: a quiet reading room, adjacent to the conservatory, holding a vast collection of antique manuscripts. No gloves, no access.
  • Conservatory: this afternoon tearoom is set within a glasshouse, filled with greenery and overlooking the hotel’s main garden.
  • Auditorium: a grand performance hall equipped for film screenings, concerts, theatrical productions, opera performances and private projections.
  • Casino.
  • Aquarium.
  • Botanical garden.
  • Pools and pool bar/restaurant.

Rooftop

  • Observatory: tempietto with a small telescope.
  • Roof garden.
  • Terraces.
  • Rooftop plaza.
