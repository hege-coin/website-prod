Level 1 of the Hege Story

As his first night at the Hegilton goes off the rails, Hege must now defy the odds and prove all his doubters wrong.

Spoiler Alert! This entry is about Level 1 of the Hege Story. If you haven’t read it yet, do so first!

Read the story

Description

Level 1 of The Hege Story starts a few months after the class trip to the planetarium in Level 0.

After the planetarium, things seemed to warm up between Hege and Hegena. But then they turned cold again for no apparent reason, and Hege is left sad and confused. Searching for something new — and fuelled by a desperate need for funds after the Casino Incident — Hege gets a job as a waiter at the elegant Hegilton Hotel.

Walking up to the entrance, Hege feels like he’s at a turning point in his life. “This will be the start of something new”, he thinks to himself.

But, as often is the case, things don’t exactly go as planned, and Hege is left scrambling after his first shift goes off the rails.
