The Iron Paw

The no. 1 destination for gym bros, fitness rookies, and hot yoga moms.

Description

The Iron Paw is a gym in Hegeville.

Previously it was the exclusive domain of Hendrix's and Ricky's crew. However, it then reached mainstream success due to its exceptional social media PR. Much to the crew’s chagrin, the gym is now popular among pros and beginners alike, including Hege.

Hendrix especially isn’t too pleased with the gym’s newfound popularity, as he now has to share his sanctum with all the other pipsqueaks. His contempt for the weak is only kept at bay by all the opportunities he now has to intimidate other gym goers and flex his pecs in the general direction of hot yoga moms.

History

The Iron Paw was inspired by the DRiP set Hege: Rep by Rep.

It then featured in the chapter Dumb and Dumbbell, when Ricky advised a romantically frustrated Hendrix.
