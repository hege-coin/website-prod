Reyna

Foxy is an understatement.

Details

Age
17
Height
156 cm (5'1")
Weight
55 kg (121 lbs)
Family
Twin sister
A litter of younger siblings
Occupation
Senior waiter at the The Hegilton Hotel
First appearance
A New Start

Character

Reyna works in the restaurant at The Hegilton Hotel as a senior waiter.

She is confident and quick on her feet, with an easy charm that tends to get her through situations without much effort.

Quick-witted and observant, she rarely seems caught off guard, even when things around her become hectic. She knows how to read a room, knows when to speak up, and seems to have a knack for getting what she wants without making a big deal out of it.

Reyna has been at the Hegilton long enough to know that self preservation is part of the job, and by now that’s second nature to her.

Relationship to Hege

Reyna took Hege under her proverbial wing when he started working at the Hegilton, showing him the routines, unwritten rules, and little tricks that come with the job.

With her experience and most of all her sharp instincts, she quickly becomes someone Hege can rely on, steering him in the right direction while giving him room to find his own footing.

Her experience at the Hegilton makes her one of the more capable people around Hege during his early days there, and their friendship develops alongside his growing familiarity with the hotel.

Trivia

Reyna’s first non-canon appearance dates back to 2 December 2025, with her debut in the True Grit DRiP drop as a “trainer of the month” poster at the Iron Paw gym.

She shows up again in the Form Check drop where she’s seen attending the front desk at the gym, where she was initially meant to work as aerobics teacher and receptionist.

She likes spicy food, listens to prog-rock (no big deal) and did shrooms once. Once.
