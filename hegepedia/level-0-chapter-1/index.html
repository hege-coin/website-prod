Description

Prickly Past, Punctured Heart is the first chapter of Level 0 of The Hege Story.

Synopsis

This chapter introduces Hege, the main character of the Hege Story.

Hege is a young hedgehow who had a rough start in life, as his parent were victims of a car accident. This turned Hege into a shy old-school romatic, nursing dreams about his crush Hegena in secret.

But one day, Hendrix discovers Hege’s secret diary, and learns of his secret love for Hegena. Not one to miss the opportunity to bully somebody weaker than himself, Hendrix makes a mockery of Hege, who is left devastated.

Hegena herself doesn’t mind Hege admiring her, but he’s also too meek to be of much interest to her.
