Description

Hegena’s Birthday Party is the third chapter in Level 0 of The Hege Story.

Synopsis

Hege is invited to Hegena's birthday party at the casino and he sees this as the perfect chance to prove himself.

Showing up in his newly thrifted tuxedo, the evening gets off to a promising start as he garners respect at the poker table.

But things take a turn for the worse when Hendrix decides to play dirty, leading to what came to be known as The Casino Incident (see below).
