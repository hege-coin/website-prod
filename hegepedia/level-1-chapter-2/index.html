Off on the Wrong Foot (L1.C2)

In the second chapter of the Hege Story, Hege's first shift at the Hegilton goes from bad to worse.

Level 1, Chapter 2

Chapter name
Off on the Wrong Foot
Release date
25 October 2026
Previous chapter
A New Start (L1.C1)
Next chapter
-

Description

In this second chapter of The Hege Story, we get to follow Hege’s first shift as a waiter in the restaurant at The Hegilton Hotel.

Hege’s evening gets a bad start right off the bat, as he disovers that he is serving Hendrix, who is out on a date with Hege’s long time crush Hegena.

This unsettles Hege deeply and he struggles to pull himself together all through the evening. Reyna supports him and helps him muddle through, but in the end the evening ends in disaster anyway, when Hendrix orders the infamous Hegilton Chocolate Fountain.

As Hege is carrying this monstrosity to the couple’s table, he slips on the wet floor, launching the dessert straight up in the air. (The floor is wet because Hege himself spilled water on it earlier during the evening and never cleaned it up).

Notable Appearances

There were no new charaters introducted in this chapter, but The Hegilton Chocolate Fountain was seen for the first time.

This chapter also introduced The Hegepedia, with a pop-up featuring an excerpt with details about the chocolate fountain.

Release

The chapter was released on the HEGE website and announced in Hegequarters on 25 October 2026.
