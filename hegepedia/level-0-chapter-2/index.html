The Pursuit of Hegena’s Hegina is the second chapter in Level 0 of The Hege Story.

Synopsis

After being humiliated by Hendrix, Hege now sets off on his quest to make a name for himself in the world of cryptocurrency memecoins.

To Hege’s detriment however, all his hard work and many sleepless nights don’t pay off much — he still doesn’t amount to anything compared to Hendrix, who has both charm and money in plentiful supply.
