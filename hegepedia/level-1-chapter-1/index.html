A New Start (L1.C1)

In this inaugural chapter of the Hege Story, the doors are not what they seem.

Level 1, Chapter 1

Chapter name
A New Start
Release date
4 October 2026
Previous chapter
-
Next chapter
Off on the Wrong Foot (L1.C2)

Description

In this first chapter of The Hege Story, Hege comes to work his first shift as a waiter in the restaurant at The Hegilton Hotel.

He misunderstands the directions from the sloth at the reception, knocks at the wrong door, and is then embarrassed by Reyna, who has her first canon appearance in this chapter.

As it turns out, Reyna is also working in the restaurant, and the chapter ends with her inviting Hege to follow her along.

Release

This chapter was released on the HEGE website and announced in Hegequarters on 4 October 2026. [1]

The day after, on 5 October 2026, it was announced on X. [2]
