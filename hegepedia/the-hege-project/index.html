The HEGE Project

Starting out as a simple memecoin on Solana in early 2024, the HEGE project has since grown into a full brand, backed by a grass-roots community of dedicated believers.

Team

HEGE is run by a diverse group of people from all over the world, all volunteering their time and effort to make the vision come alive.

History

The origins of HEGE go back to early 2024.

At that time, the memecoin space was going absolutely bonkers. From being a relatively niche thing even within crypto, memecoins were now THE thing, and everybody started launching coins left and right to cash in on the hype.

Most memecoins launching at that time were cash grabs, plain and simple, investors being rugged by founders within weeks, days, or even hours. It was an awful environment for new people coming into the crypto space, and exactly this observation sparked the creation of HEGE.

Hege Dev — who came up with the idea and launched the coin — wanted to start a project where he could use his skills as a graphic designer towards something better: a safe haven for both new and experienced memecoin enthusiasts alike.

He got the idea for a coin centered around a hedgehog after talking to his girlfriend. He thought it was a playful and fun critique of the state of the economy, market manipulation, and hedge funds. One thing led to another, and after recieving some initial positive feedback, on 23 March 2024 he wrote a simple Reddit post asking for help launching his coin. [1]

As it turned out, others resonated with his idea — much more so than he expected — and over the next two weeks, over 150 contributors raised $32k to the initial $HEGE liquidity pool. On 2 April 2026, the coin was launched on Raydium, with a supply of 1 billion $HEGE and an initial market cap of $64k. [2]

The rest, as they say, is history.
