Helena

Helena is Hegena's eccentric mother, and part of the main cast of the Hege Story.

Description

Helena is a very protective parent, and her main objective throughout the story is to have her daughter Hegena marry into money and secure a comfortable life for the both of them.

In her head, her daughter’s future is pretty much Hendrix-shaped.

Her goofy character provides comic relief in a variety of situations as she’s often seen working odd jobs in different places, while often being disastrously bad at them.

Trivia

Helena is unjustifiably bad at every new job she takes on despite having a wide and, albeit niche, very advanced skillset.

Some of her canon feats include but are not limited to:

  • Being the national Krav Maga running champion 7 years in a row.
  • Possessing surgical ice sculpting precision.
  • Advanced CRISPR engineering and surviving as Class-D personnel at the SCP foundation for 14 months before she got bored and resigned.

She deliberately chose the odd-job life because she thinks it’s more fun.

Helena also possesses an IQ of 133 and is forklift certified, and even the authors are stumped at her mysterious incompetence.

Approach with caution.
