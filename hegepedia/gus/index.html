Description

Gus is the newly appointed head of the dining room at The Hegilton Hotel. His job is to make sure that every single evening runs like clockwork.

From guest relations to floor management and from staff supervision to menu curation, a flawless night boils down to Gus executing his job impeccably.

He is Hege's and Reyna's direct superior, and during his still brief time at the Hegilton he has already clocked who he can safely rely on when his floor gets hectic. Likewise, Reyna has him pretty much figured out: being on time means you’re late, don’t look lost, don’t piss him off.

Gus’ military background carries over to how he runs things inside and outside of his workplace. He sticks to a strict routine, appreciates a clear chain of command and doesn’t take shit from anybody.

When it comes to fight or flight, he is a flightless bird.

Trivia

Gus served in the military as Sergeant First Class for a long time and he was well underway for a promotion to E-8.

He had been eyeing the well-deserved move from drill Sergeant routines to the administrative roles of a Master Sergeant for a while, and he was already looking forward to having more breathing room to indulge his maîtrise passion.

But Gus wasn’t the only one savoring the prospect of a promotion. A fellow candidate had other plans, deliberately exposing Gus’ private life under Don’t Ask, Don’t Tell to eliminate the competition, claim the promotion for themselves, and bring Gus’ career to an abrupt end.

Gus’ military discharge played a big role in making him the unfuckwithable leader he is today. He experienced the glass ceiling first-hand and worked twice as hard than anyone else all his life. He takes immense pride in his hard-earned position at the Hegilton and protects it with a passion.

He swore to himself he won’t let anyone take what’s his ever again.
