Hendrix

Hendrix is one of the main characters in the Hege Story. He is Hege's main antagonist, primarily because he is Hegena's boyfriend.

Description

Hendrix comes from a wealthy background and has built his identity around his lifestyle, appearance, and status.

He is a self-obsessed bully who strongly identifies with the “alpha” mentality, at the expense of everyone around him (and sometimes even to his own expense.)

Hendrix spends much of his time working out and maintaining his physique, and is rarely shy about showing it off. He carries himself with an air of superiority and tends to view the people around him through a hierarchy of who has more money, influence, or social standing. He is particularly dismissive toward those he considers beneath him.

More often than not his plastic confidence spills over into arrogance, and he has little patience for weakness or vulnerability. Hendrix can be charming when it suits him, and he knows how to use that charm to manipulate others to get what he wants. He has a chauvinistic attitude toward women and tends to treat relationships as another extension of status and control.

Hendrix frequently targets Hege, taking advantage of their very different social positions to belittle and intimidate him. His relationship with Hegena puts him even closer to Hege’s life, despite the hostility between the two.

Although Hendrix presents himself as completely self-assured, his behavior around other men occasionally reveals cracks in that image.

Trivia

Most of Hendrix’s wealth is money from the allowance he receives from his father.

He is very self conscious about his friend Ricky being bigger than he is.
