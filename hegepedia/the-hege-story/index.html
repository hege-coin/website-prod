The Hege Story

The Greatest Underhog Story Ever Told

The Hege Story is an ongoing webcomic following the young hedgehog Hege.

Set in a world populated by anthropomorphic animals, the story follows Hege as he meets new people, forms friendships and relationships, and finds himself caught up in the events of everyday life.

On his mission to become a successful meme and win over the heart of Hegena, what begins as a relatively ordinary story gradually expands into a larger narrative, introducing new elements and harder challenges as Hege’s journey continues.

Events from earlier chapters carry forward into later parts of the story, with characters and details returning and developing over time.

The story is still ongoing, with new chapters releasing every month.

Levels and Chapters

The Hege Story is organized into Levels and Chapters. [1]

You can think of the levels kind of like levels in a video game. Entering a new level means that Hege will have levelled up as a character and that he’s now unlocking a new world, with new environments, new adventures, and new support characters.

From week to week, these levels are told in chapters, each chapter consisting of a short illustrated story.
