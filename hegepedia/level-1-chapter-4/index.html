Spare Me Your Pronouns

Description

Spare Me Your Pronouns is the fourth chapter of Level 1 of The Hege Story.

Synopsis

In this chapter, Hege has to deal with the aftermath of dropping The Hegilton Chocolate Fountain.

In Chapter 2, Hege attempted a free-hand delivery of the Chocolate Fountain to the table where his rival Hendrix and his crush Hegena sit on a date. Predictably, this ends in disaster, as Hege slips and sends the fountain airborne.

This chapter starts with Hege coming to his senses under the couple’s table. In front of him, he’s got Hegena’s legs, displayed in all their glory. Understandably, Hege is transfixed.

Hege’s trance is quickly broken, however, as Reyna hauls him out from under the table and banishes him to the kitchen, ASAP.

In the kitchen, Hege meets the fury of Gus, the newly hired maître. Gus chews Hege up properly, until Hege reels out through the back door into the alley behind the restaurant.

Hege is sitting in the alley, trying to make sense of what just happens, when Hendrix shows up. Hendrix spotted Hege from his car as he and Hegena were about to leave, and will not waste the opportunity to put Hege in his place.

Hendrix approaches Hege, grabs him by the collar, and starts berating him.

By this point, Hege is completely overwhelmed and simply cannot process what’s going on. All his sense of self-preservation is gone, and he taps into a confidence he did not know he possessed. Without fear, he talks back to Hendrix, ridiculing his smudged white shirt.

Hendrix, of course, does not take well to this, and for the second time this evening, everything goes black for Hege.

Notable Appearances

This chapter introduced Gus, the maître at the Hegilton Restaurant.

The chapter was released on the HEGE website and announced in Hegequarters on 22 November 2026. [1]
