The Planetarium Class Trip

The in-lore moment when Hege decides to officially launch his eponymous project HEGE.

Description

As told in The Class Trip, during a class trip to the Planetarium, Hendrix's usual antics took an unexpected turn when his girlfriend Hegena finally stood up to him.

After Hendrix took another jab at Hege, Hegena gave him a taste of his own medicine. She exposed his illiteracy, hair-loss medication, and his dependence on her help with homework (among many other things) in front of the entire class. With Hendrix thoroughly humiliated, Hegena decided to sit on the bus next to Hege instead.

The two got to know each other better during the trip, with Hegena opening up about her late father and the values she shared with him: integrity, kindness and looking out for others over money and material possessions. Hege was completely smitten, imagining a future where he had finally made something of himself, with Hegena by his side.

Reality came crashing back when Hendrix interrupted Hege’s daydreaming, making it clear that he still considered Hegena his girlfriend and had no intention of letting Hege get between them. But Hege’s path was now already laid out before him: he would become a man worthy of Hegena’s love.

“And so, as the bus disappeared into the twilight, Hege embarked on a new and more confident chapter that would change his life forever. Fueled by integrity, courage and a touch of stardust he vowed to create a platform where everyone could shine; because sometimes it’s not the brightest stars that illuminate the way, it’s the ones with the most heart.”

— The Narrator
