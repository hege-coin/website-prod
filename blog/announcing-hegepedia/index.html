Announcing Hegepedia

Today we’re excited to introduce a new section on the Hege website: The Hegepedia — your ultimate guide to everything HEGE.

by Team Hege

Announcement / October 25, 2026

Hegepedia
he-ge-pe-dia
['he-jə-ˈpēd-ē-ə]
A portmanteau of the words "Hege" and "encyclopedia", the Hegepedia is the ultimate guide to the Hegeverse.
— The Hegepedia

We Have A Lot of Content

Over the years we’ve produced a lot of great content, but it’s always been difficult to find.

It’s been scattered across many different places — amazing artwork has been hidden away collecting dust in a Google drive folder, articles have been languishing on Reddit, and deep community lore has only existed in the brains of OG Hegends.

This sorry state of affairs ends right now.

Now It Finally Has a Home

As of today, we finally welcome all this scattered content into a home worthy of its presence: The Hegepedia.

The Hegepedia will be the one place where all our stories, lore, and artwork come together. It will be THE place to go to for anything related to the Hege Story and the HEGE project.

Starting out small, it will cover the very basics. Then over time it will grow alongside the project, gradually being filled with more and more content as the Hege Universe expands, standing as an ever evolving monument to the shared community effort that the HEGE project is.

Safe to say we’re really excited about the new possibilities this opens up.
