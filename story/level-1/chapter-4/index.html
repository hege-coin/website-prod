Spare Me Your Pro­nouns

Hege feels his eyelids twitch. They're sticky and hard to open.

His mouth is all sticky too. It tastes sweet.

Coming to his senses, Hege realizes he's now under the table.

He must have fallen somehow.

Outside, it's chocolate mayhem.

But here under the table, only one thing exists.

HEGENA'S LEGS.

She's wearing a pair of shiny stilettos, pointier than his own quills.

Hege is transfixed.

Suddenly the black marble tiles start to move under him.

It's Reyna again.

Her voice snaps him ouf of his trance as she rips him from the floor.

"Kitchen. NOW!"

In shock, Hege stumbles towards the kitchen.

The entire restaurant is looking at him.

But he's too dazed to notice.

"Do you have ANY idea what you've just done?"

The voice hits Hege in the face before he's even made it to the back of the kitchen.

"Are you actively trying to sabotage me? Do you know who his father is? What the HELL was that, exactly!?"

The maître looks like he's about to explode.

"I... He... They..." Hege starts.

The maître interrupts him.

"Oh spare me your pronouns. I'm frutier than the peach macédoine you just flung at my patrons."

Hege just stands there, slowly blinking.

Then the maître explodes.

"Do you have any idea what I've been through to get where I am now? How hard I've fought to earn my place at the Hegilton? Allow me to clue you in: harder than anyone! And I'll be damned if I'll let you undo all those years of work and dedication. I won't allow anyone to undermine me anymore. I want you off my floor, now. DO YOU HEAR ME? OFF! MY! FLOOR! NOW!"

Shell-shocked, Hege reels towards the nearest exit.

It's a backdoor, leading out to the alley.

It's dark outside now.

Dim light filters in from the street.

A cat disappears into the shadows.

Hege's mind is blank.

It all happened so fast it seems unreal.

Around the corner, a doors slams.

Muffled voices.

They sound angry.

"Hendrix!"

Hegena's voice echoes from back there somewhere.

But Hendrix doesn't hear.

He's completely focused on Hege as he makes his way over.

As he arrives, he raises his arm and grabs Hege by the collar.

"Listen here you little shitstain. There are two kinds of people in this world. The servers and the served, and YOU serve ME."

"Look at you, all meek and weird. Did you really think a nobody like you would EVER have a chance with HER? Pathetic."

Hendrix's voice curiously fades into the background.

Hege can't focus.

All he can see is the big chocolate smudge on the expensive white shirt.

"You are NOTHING to her. And you better stay the hell away from her if you know what's good for you. You got that under your stupid ass quills?"

Hege can't contain a nervous twitter now.

He has completely lost control.

"And just what the hell are you laughing about?"

Hege giggles.

It feels like his voice is coming from somebody else now.

"Nothing it's just... I'M the shitstain? Have you seen your shirt? AHSHAHAHJA!"

Black again.
